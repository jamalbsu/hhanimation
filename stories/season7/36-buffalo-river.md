## The story in one breath

A buffalo herd charges the pack head-on through a shallow river and, in the chaos, loses its own calf to the current. The Jaguar dives in and saves it. Then the herd comes back even angrier, because from where Mama stands the cats have stolen her baby. Running away would prove her right. So the pack runs *toward* the horns, so the calf can be seen, and the herd that tried to flatten them ends up running at their side.

**What the viewer should feel, in order:** shock (0–3 s) → worry for the calf → a laugh → dread when the hooves come back → a held breath at the wall of horns → relief → warmth.

**The lesson, never said out loud:** courage isn't running fast. It's running *to* the problem.

## The world

- **Place:** the last river in the savanna, wide and shallow, at sunrise. The whole episode happens in the water; the pack doesn't reach dry ground until about 23 s.
- **Show the drought without a word:** the water is ankle-to-chest deep; pale sandbars break the surface; the banks carry a dark high-water line well above the river; the mud at the edges is cracked.
- **Palette:** sky `#4a78b8` → `#f0a868` → `#ffe2b0`. Water `#4f9aa0` and `#3c7c84` with gold sun glitter. Banks `#8a6e48`. Warm grade `#ffa040` at about 0.12. Spray is white-gold and backlit; it's the visual signature of the episode.
- **Light:** low sun ahead and to one side, so the herd arrives as dark shapes inside glowing spray.
- **Start from:** the `savanna` biome and episode 31's river (`w: 11500`), made long enough to last the episode.

## The three shots that must land

Spend your effort here. If these three work, the episode works.

1. **The hook (0.4–1.0 s).** The frame at 0.9 s is the thumbnail: a wall of horns bursting out of white backlit spray, the pack small and dark in front of it. Let the herd be visible as shapes from the very first frame, so the first second is already a threat, and let the pop land as the horns come clear.
2. **The second charge (11.8–16.2 s).** It has to read as *the same herd, coming back, angry at the cats*. Three things sell it: Mama's horn mark in front, her bubble, and the calf sitting in plain sight on the Jaguar. Keep the calf visible in every shot from 8.2 to 17.4; it is the whole payoff. The pack accelerates into this charge. It must not look like they have no choice; the Black Panther's line is a decision.
3. **The stop (15.0–17.4 s).** Hold the silence for a full second before it. Then one frame-filling curtain of water, and when it falls the herd is standing still, noses almost touching the cats. Use a low side camera so horns and cats share the frame. This is the only moment in the episode where anything is still.

## Music and sound

- Minor, driving, about 120 bpm: `EPIC(36, 57, 120)` is the starting point.
- Three hits in `flashes`: the hook (about 0.45), the twist (about 11.85) and the stop (16.2). The stop is the biggest, so the silence belongs before it.
- From 19.0 the score lifts into the major-feeling triumph for the escort.
- Mama and the herd speak in the big voice. The calf, riding, speaks in the small voice.

## What exists and what's new

**Reuse:** the buffalo; a head-on herd with `spray` (ep 31 hippos); a head-on herd that brakes to a stop in front of the pack (ep 34's second boar herd); hippo and flamingos as background; the river; riders; the top-down dodge camera from ep 31 at 2.1 s.

**New:**
- A **calf sprite** for `riders`, and a short "drifting in the current" state before it's picked up. Episode 31's otter on a log (`logs` with a `rider`, then `riders`) is the nearest thing.
- **One cat leaving formation for a beat** (the Jaguar's dive and return). The pack's lanes are fixed today.
- **A rider on a cat other than the lead with its own speech bubble.** `back: true` only anchors to the lead's rider.
- **The escort.** The stopped herd can't reverse. Swap in a `dir: 1` herd on both sides of the pack, hidden by the water curtain or a colour wipe.
- **Mama's horn mark**, and oxpeckers that sit on backs and then fly off.

## Don't

- Don't add crocodiles, a waterfall or any second danger. The only threat is the herd.
- Don't let the pack turn around or slow down for the second charge.
- Don't hide the calf behind spray or a bubble during 15.0–16.2 s.
- Don't let the buffalo touch the cats. They stop short.
- Don't end on dry land with the herd gone. The escort is the reward shot.
