About the cartoon

Panthers Pack is an animated cartoon series from Happy Hillz about four big cats who never stop running: a black panther who leads the way, a quiet second black panther, a leopard and a jaguar. Each episode is a short animation with one huge problem and one clever way through it. Elephants charge, rivers flood, stars fall out of the sky, and the pack makes friends with zebras, giraffes and hippos along the way.

Every frame is drawn in code and every score is generated, not recorded. The same episode you can watch on YouTube is running live in the phone at the top of this page. Watch the vertical Shorts on your phone or the landscape versions on a big screen. The series is family-friendly, and a new episode comes out every week.
