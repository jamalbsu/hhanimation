How to play

  1. 1

    Switch lanes. Swipe left or right on the game. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or A and D.

  2. 2

    Jump. Tap the game or swipe up. On a keyboard, press Space or the up arrow. Jump over logs and boulders. Elephants and buffalo are too big to jump, so go around them.

  3. 3

    Grab the stars. Every star is worth 25 points, on top of a point for every meter the pack runs.

  4. 4

    Last 45 seconds. Make it to the end for 200 bonus points. Three bumps and the round is over.

Three threats, one pack

Every round follows the rule of the cartoon: one huge thing comes straight at the pack. Pick an elephant charge, a buffalo stampede or a rockslide of rolling boulders before you run. Each one comes from an episode you can watch: {{threatEpisodes}}.

About the game

Pack Run is a free online game starring the {{series}}: the black panther who leads the way, the second panther, the leopard and the jaguar. It runs in your browser on a phone, a tablet or a computer, with no download, no sign-up and no ads. The cats are drawn live by the same animation code as the cartoon, so they gallop and leap exactly like they do in the episodes. Sound starts off; turn it on with the speaker button for the music and the elephants.

To count how many people play, the game keeps a random number in your browser and sends it when a round starts and ends. It isn't linked to a name, an account or a cookie, and nothing else about you is collected.
